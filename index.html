Managing several MetaTrader 5 accounts can mean opening, checking and switching between multiple terminals. FlowMetrix brings the configured accounts and terminal workflows into a centralized Windows dashboard.

Multiple MT5 accounts in one place

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about FlowMetrix and the public release.

What is FlowMetrix?

FlowMetrix is a Windows desktop application for managing multiple MetaTrader 5 accounts and terminals from a centralized dashboard.

Can FlowMetrix manage multiple MT5 accounts?

Yes. FlowMetrix is designed around multi-account MT5 management and provides a single dashboard for configured accounts and associated terminal workflows.

Does FlowMetrix support copy trading?

FlowMetrix includes multi-account trade-copying workflows. Exact behavior depends on the configured accounts and the version you are running.

What is an MT5 multi-account manager?

An MT5 multi-account manager is software that helps organize and operate multiple MetaTrader 5 accounts or terminals from a centralized workflow. FlowMetrix is designed for this Windows use case.

Can I manage multiple MetaTrader 5 terminals?

Yes. FlowMetrix is designed to manage configured MT5 terminals from a centralized desktop dashboard rather than requiring every terminal to be handled independently.
